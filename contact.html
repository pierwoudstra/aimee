Aimée Phillips develops lexicons of forms, materials and text(ures) in an anti-disciplinary manner. Their artistic practise encompasses installation, sculpture, costume-making, performance, sound and writing and is strongly informed by cross-disciplinary collaborations.

Their work is shaped by an interest in the grotesque and abject, cripness, metabolic fictions and anatomical diagrams, the swelling and deflating of time and place, peripheral view, exercises in place-making through rebelry and revolting and subversive architectures and their choreo-political aspects. They see their sculptural works as active embodiments that work through intersecting and diffracting polyvocal epistemologies.

Gestures of inflating, deflating, expanding, shrinking and contracting reappear in their work frequently and are part of their costumes as kinetic elements. Of recent they have been exploring fabricating sculptural suits that cover the entire body. The suits restrict and permit movement and this forms the basis for collaborative performances, installations and films. The sculptural suits merge out of relation and social processes rather than a fixed state or concept and are in an active interaction with the surroundings they find themselves in.

Aimée Phillips holds a BA in Text and Textile from the Gerrit Rietveld Academy Amsterdam (2023) and a MA from the Dutch Art Institute (2026). They have exhibited and performed at the W139, Textiel Museum Tilburg, PAKT, Kunstpodium-T, Kunstfort Vijfhuizen and Stedelijk Museum Amsterdam. And have been an artist in residence at AADK (ES, 2024), Culterim Gallery (DE, 2024) and GlogauAIR (DE, 2027).
