Can we grow organs for the alternative? 2025
Presentation at Performing Arts Forum, France




Can we grow organs for the alternative? is tableaux vivant exploring conceptions of futurity through a deep affiliation with the book 'The Hawthorn Archive: letters from the Utopian Margins' (2017) by Avery F. Gordon. Gordon takes up Marcuse’s notion on subjectivity in liberation — the idea that revolution and change require not only structural or political transformation but also a change in people’s subjectivities;
in what they feel, what they want and how they sense the world.
